We take a hard-constrained route for inverse diffusion. A neural field parameterises only a strictly positive diffusion coefficient, while option-like prices are produced by a differentiable Galerkin time stepper. Positivity, coercivity, and SPD of every implicit layer are properties of the architecture rather than penalties. The resulting sigma-Q operator is differentiated through its SPD solves. We prove a no-go proposition: no finite soft penalty weight can certify physical admissibility.
