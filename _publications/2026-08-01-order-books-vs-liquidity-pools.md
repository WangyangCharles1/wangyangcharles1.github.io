We provide the first fully real-data comparison of four market-structure paradigms coexisting for ETH. Our synchronized 1-minute panel with 177,872 decoded on-chain swaps shows a sharp mechanism ordering in execution cost. Centralized order books dominate price discovery (79% permanent component). We exploit two quasi-experiments and provide a transparent MEV analysis.
